# MARINE MADNESS
================

In this game, you play as Sgt. Ronald McDonald on a quest to retrieve a stolen "alternative physics" device.

But the device is faulty and opens a portal that sends you across 3 distinct levels, representing the prehistoric, western and future time eras.

For each level, there is a suitable enemy e.g. the western level has dastardly cowboy bandits.

Your objective is to progress through each level, hoping that the next warp sends you back to the present.

Good luck out there brave soldier!
